BATTLELAB — BATTLE RULES
Updated: 4 October 2026

PURPOSE
Record the current battle rules and keep unresolved design choices separate.
This document takes precedence over older proposals for simultaneous move
selection in the README and starter-code comments.

1. TEAM SELECTION
- A match has two players.
- Each player selects three different characters from the ten-character roster.
- Both teams are locked in before the battle starts.

2. TEAM REVEAL
- The playable single-player prototype reveals both teams when the battle
  begins.
- Revealing the team does not reveal future actions.

3. ACTIVE CHARACTERS
- Each player fields one active character at a time.
- Their other two characters begin in reserve.
- Combat is one active character against one active character.

4. TURNS AND ACTIONS
- Players take alternating turns.
- A player takes one action on their turn: use a move or voluntarily switch.
- The prototype has the human move first.
- The bot responds automatically after the human action.

5. CHARACTER MOVES AND TYPES
- Each character has four fixed moves defined in backend/roster.py.
- Magic beats Physical; Physical beats Spirit; Spirit beats Magic.
- Strong attacks deal 125% damage; resisted attacks deal 80%; same type is neutral.
- Move type, not the user's character type, determines the matchup.
- Signature effects, exact durations, numbers and resolution order are documented
  in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-character-identities-design.md.
- Conditions are deterministic. Offensive statuses deal a small initial hit.
- No energy, cooldowns, random turn skips, speed order or move priority.

6. VOLUNTARY SWITCHING
- On their turn, a player may switch to another non-knocked-out character on
  their team.
- Switching uses the player's turn; the player cannot also use a move.
- Switching clears temporary effects and guard; paralysis blocks voluntary
  switching for one action but never blocks free knockout replacement.

7. KNOCKOUTS AND FREE REPLACEMENT
- A character is knocked out when its health reaches zero and cannot continue
  fighting or be selected as a replacement.
- If the active character is knocked out and the player has eligible reserves,
  that player chooses which reserve to deploy.
- This forced replacement is free and does not consume a turn.
- After replacement, normal turn order continues.

8. VICTORY
- A player wins when all three opposing characters have been knocked out.
- If both final characters fall in the same action, the acting player loses.
- If the defeated player has no eligible replacement, the match ends.

9. DECISIONS STILL TO MAKE
- Final balancing of character attributes, move values, healing amounts, and
  guard strength after playtesting.
- Whether further move effects belong in later releases.
- Rules for disconnects, inactivity, surrender, and any possible draw.
- Multiplayer turn synchronization and persistent session storage.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
The current browser prototype uses alternating turns and a server-authoritative
battle API. The repository's README includes historical proposals; follow the
current playable rules documented here for battle behavior.
